F.A.Q

Frequently Asked Questions

01

What services does Hofileña Hospital offer?

Hofileña Hospital provides a wide range of healthcare services, including Emergency Care, Outpatient Consultation, Laboratory Services, Radiology and Imaging, Pharmacy Services, Inpatient Care, Surgery, and Specialized Medical Services.

02

How long is the waiting time for appointments?

Our Emergency Room operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Outpatient clinics and other departments may have specific schedules. Please contact the hospital for updated clinic hours.

03

How can I schedule an appointment?

Appointments may be scheduled by visiting the hospital, calling our reception desk, or through our official online channels, if available.

04

Do you accept health insurance and HMOs?

Yes. Hofileña Hospital accepts selected health insurance providers and HMOs. Please contact our billing department to verify your coverage.

05

What should I bring during my hospital visit?

Please bring a valid ID, referral documents (if any), previous medical records, insurance or HMO cards, and any prescribed medications you are currently taking.
